Google Drive Access

The application requests Google Drive access to read, create, update, move, and delete files used in the developer's personal automation workflow. The OAuth permission allows access to Google Drive, although the configured workflow targets designated folders.

Files are stored in the developer's Google Drive and may be processed on the developer's private server. Connected AI services may access relevant files when explicitly used by the developer.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information or use Google Drive data for advertising.

Access and Revocation

Google Drive access can be revoked through the Google Account security settings.
